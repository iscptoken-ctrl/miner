List a token

Anyone can add a new token. Cost: 10 CD1, paid to the creator's wallet. CD1 is the creator's own token.

  • Tokens with a transfer tax cannot be listed. The contract sends a small test amount of your token in and straight back to you. If any of it goes missing, listing is rejected and nothing is charged.
  • You get the full test amount back. You need at least a tiny balance of the token.
  • Listing is permanent and is not an endorsement. Community tokens are marked community.

How it works

  • A seller locks tokens in the contract and names exactly what they want in return.
  • Anyone can fill the whole order. The seller gets exactly their price. No pools, no slippage.
  • Fee: 0.05% from the seller and 0.05% from the buyer, each taken from what they receive. No other fees.
  • Sellers can cancel anytime, free, with a full refund. Orders never expire.
  • The contract has no owner and no admin. Nobody, including the creator, can change rules, pause it, or touch funds.
  • Tokens with transfer taxes are rejected, both when listing and when trading.

Core and community tokens

Core tokens were set when the contract was deployed. Anyone can add more for 10 CD1 (paid to the creator; CD1 is the creator's own token). Community-listed tokens are not checked by anyone. Fake copies of popular tokens are possible. Always check the contract address. This site may hide reported tokens from its lists; the contract keeps working for them.

Binance-Peg tokens

Many tokens on BNB Chain (ETH, XRP, ADA, DOT and others) are "Binance-Peg" versions, issued by Binance and backed by the original coins it holds. They are not the native coins of those chains.

Where prices come from

Prices on this site are read directly from PancakeSwap V2 pools and converted to USD through USDT. They are indicative only. Pools with low liquidity can be moved easily and may show wrong prices; these are marked thin. The contract does not use these prices. Trades happen only at the exact amounts written in each order.

Your responsibility

This is an interface to an immutable smart contract. Before creating or filling an order, check the token addresses, amounts and price yourself. Displayed prices may be delayed or wrong. Transactions on the blockchain are final and cannot be reversed. Use at your own risk. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
