Own money is the USDT a player put in from their own wallet; earnings spent again on upgrades and repairs are not counted twice. Net profit = everything earned minus everything spent (claimed or not). Return = (own money + net profit) ÷ own money, so 200% means the player doubled their money. CD1 spent is shown separately. 1 Sat = 0.00000001 USDT. Demo mode: balances, 200 other players and CD1 dividends are simulated. The contract starts with 10,000 CD1. CD1 has a supply of 450,000 and at $0.42 pays holders about 45 USDT per day in total (between 22 and 68 in the demo); the contract earns its share, about 1 USDT per day per 10,000 CD1. CD1 price moves on its own in the demo: it starts at $0.42 and rises by $2 for every 2,000 CD1 used in the game. Dividends are assumed to rise in line with the price. Simulated players spend CD1 on electricity, technicians and overclock. USDT payments send 99% to the pool and 1% to the owner. CD1 payments stay in the contract.

How to play

The goal

You run a mining facility. Your GPUs produce hash power, and the reward pool pays out 3% of itself every day, split between all miners by their share of the network's hash power. More effective hash means a bigger share.

What is a Sat?

Rewards are counted in Sat, like Bitcoin. 1 Sat = 0.00000001 USDT, so 100,000,000 Sat = 1 USDT. Rewards build up every second and you claim them whenever you like.

How to grow

  1. Fill your free slots with GPUs and upgrade them.
  2. When a limit is hit, the Capacity box tells you which one and what to buy.
  3. Upgrade power (PSU and electricity) before high-level GPUs, since they draw more watts.
  4. Keep heat under your cooling capacity, or you lose hash.
  5. Claim your Sat and reinvest, or take it out.

Where your payments go

99% of every USDT payment goes straight into the reward pool; 1% goes to the game owner. CD1 payments stay in the contract, and the USDT dividends that CD1 earns also feed the pool.

Returns are not guaranteed

What you earn depends on how many new miners join and on CD1 dividends. Miners who join early usually earn more. When fewer new miners join, late miners may not earn back what they spent. Wear after break-even slows down the earliest miners and moves part of their earnings back to the pool. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

Good to know

Your production is a live estimate. When new miners join, the network grows and your share gets smaller; when the pool grows, everyone earns more. In demo mode, balances and other players are simulated and nothing can be withdrawn.
